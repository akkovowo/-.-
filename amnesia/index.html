FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I activate my subscription?

After purchasing, you will receive a key. Join our Discord server and use the /register command with your username and key. Then use /get-loader to download the script.

Can I use amnesia on multiple PCs?

No, each subscription is tied to one HWID (hardware ID). For any HWID-related questions or issues, please contact Odyssey support.

What is the difference between Live and Alpha versions?

Live version includes all stable features. Alpha version gives you early access to experimental features and updates before they reach the stable build.

How do updates work?

Updates are delivered automatically. When you load the script, it checks for the latest version and downloads it if available. No manual action needed.

Can I get a refund?

Due to the nature of digital products, we do not offer refunds. Make sure to check the preview and features before purchasing.

What payment methods do you accept?

Buy in our Telegram bot — keys are issued automatically. We accept crypto, FunPay, SBP, Stars, and more.

Still have questions?
